What a Rajasthan tour package usually includes

Packages differ a lot, so read the inclusions before you compare prices. Most private Rajasthan tour packages include:

  • Hotel stays for every night of the trip, usually with daily breakfast
  • A private air-conditioned car with a driver for all transfers and sightseeing
  • A day-by-day sightseeing plan across the cities you choose
  • Airport or railway station pickup and drop

Items that are often not included, and should be confirmed in writing: monument entry tickets, guides, lunch and dinner, camel or jeep safaris, flights and train tickets, and tips. A low price sometimes just means fewer inclusions.

Sample Rajasthan tour package routes

Rajasthan is large, and distances between cities are long. A good itinerary keeps driving days realistic, so choose the number of days first and then the cities.

4 to 5 days: Jaipur and a short add-on

Best for a first visit or a long weekend

Spend two days in Jaipur for Amber Fort, City Palace and the old city, then add Pushkar or continue from Delhi on a Golden Triangle route. Our Royal Golden Triangle tour (Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, 5 nights / 6 days) starts from ₹14,999 per person.

6 to 7 days: Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur

Best for families and first-time Rajasthan visitors

The classic heritage trio: forts and palaces in Jaipur, the blue old city under Mehrangarh Fort in Jodhpur, and the lakes of Udaipur. Each city gets at least one full day.

9 to 10 days: the grand circuit

Best for travellers who want the desert and the lakes

Jaipur, Pushkar, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Ranakpur and Udaipur, with a desert camp night near Jaisalmer. This is the route of our Grand Rajasthan Royalty and Desert Haveli Expedition (9 nights / 10 days), which starts from ₹28,500 per person.

Cities worth including in a Rajasthan tour

  • Jaipur: Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the bazaars of the Pink City.
  • Pushkar: a small holy town around a lake, known for its camel fair in November.
  • Jodhpur: Mehrangarh Fort above the blue houses of the old city.
  • Jaisalmer: a living sandstone fort and the dunes of the Thar Desert, where most itineraries include a desert camp night.
  • Ranakpur: a large Jain temple with 1,444 carved pillars, a popular stop between Jodhpur and Udaipur.
  • Udaipur: Lake Pichola, the City Palace and sunset boat rides.

What decides the cost of a Rajasthan tour package

No two quotes are the same because these factors change the price:

  1. Number of days and cities: more days mean more hotel nights and more driving.
  2. Hotel category: budget, comfort or heritage haveli stays have very different rates.
  3. Season: prices rise from October to February, and around Diwali, Christmas and New Year.
  4. Vehicle: a sedan, an SUV like the Innova Crysta, or a larger vehicle for groups.
  5. Group size: the car is shared, so the price per person drops as the group grows.
  6. Extras: guides, safaris, tickets and meals if they are not in the base price.

Best time to visit Rajasthan

MonthsWhat to expect
October to MarchSunny days and cool evenings. The best season for sightseeing, and the busiest.
April to JuneVery hot, often above 40°C. Plan sightseeing for early morning and late afternoon. Hotel rates are lower.
July to SeptemberMonsoon. Lakes and hills turn green and crowds are thin, but some outdoor activities are limited.

Festivals such as the Pushkar Camel Fair (November) and the Jaisalmer Desert Festival (usually February) add colour, but dates change every year, so check before you book. Hotels in these towns fill up early.

Planning tips for your Rajasthan trip

  • Book early for October to February. Good heritage hotels and cars sell out first.
  • Do not squeeze too many cities into too few days. Two nights in a city is more relaxing than one.
  • Dress modestly at temples and shrines, covering shoulders and knees, and carry a scarf.
  • Carry water, sunscreen and a hat for fort visits, which involve a lot of walking and stairs.
  • Choose a private car and driver if you travel with children or elders. You set the pace and can stop when you like.
  • Add Agra if you have time. Many travellers combine Rajasthan with the Taj Mahal. See our Agra and Taj Mahal tours.

Rajasthan tour package FAQs

How many days are enough for a Rajasthan tour package?

Four to five days covers Jaipur and one nearby stop. Six to seven days fits Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur without rushing. Nine to ten days lets you add Pushkar, Jaisalmer and Ranakpur.

What is the best time to book a Rajasthan tour package?

October to March has the most comfortable weather. April to June is very hot. July to September is the monsoon, with green landscapes and fewer visitors. Book early for October to February, because hotels and cars fill up.

How much does a Rajasthan tour package cost?

The price depends on the number of days, hotel category, vehicle, travel dates and group size. As a reference, our 10-day Grand Rajasthan tour starts from ₹28,500 per person. Send your dates and cities for an exact quote.

What does a Rajasthan tour package usually include?

Most packages include hotel stays, a private air-conditioned car with driver and a sightseeing plan. Meals, monument tickets, guides, camel safaris and flights are often separate, so check the inclusions line by line.

Can a Rajasthan tour package be customised for a family?

Yes. You can change the cities, the number of days, the hotels and the pace. For families with children or senior travellers, shorter driving days and fewer fort climbs can be planned.
