Best Time to Visit Rajasthan: Month-by-Month Weather and Festival Guide

By Rangrez Holidays Travel Team · Updated October 17, 2026 · 4 min read

The best time to visit Rajasthan is October to March, when days are sunny and dry and desert nights are cool. November to February is the peak season, so it is also the busiest and most expensive. Summer (April to June) is very hot, and the monsoon (July to September) is humid with periodic rain.

The right month still depends on what you want to do: camp in the Thar Desert, spot tigers, catch a festival or travel on a budget. This guide breaks the year down so you can choose.

Festival dates follow the lunar calendar or official announcements and change every year, so confirm them before you book.

Best time by type of trip

  • Sightseeing and photography: November to February, when skies are clear and walking around forts is comfortable.
  • Desert camping at Jaisalmer: November to February. Nights are cold, so camps provide blankets and some heating, but pack warm layers.
  • Honeymoon: October to March for the lake views and boat rides in Udaipur.
  • Wildlife and tiger safaris: Ranthambore is open outside the monsoon months. Summer sightings can be good because animals gather at water holes, but the heat is severe.
  • Budget travel: April to June and July to September, when hotels cut prices.
  • Festivals: choose your month around the event, and book hotels well in advance.

Travelling in summer (April to June)

Rajasthan in summer is hard going, but it can be done. Start sightseeing at sunrise, rest in an air-conditioned hotel or car through the middle of the day, and carry water. A hotel with a pool makes a big difference. Many travellers prefer to avoid these months, but fares and room rates drop sharply.

Why consider the monsoon (July to September)?

Rain cools the desert, turns the Aravalli hills green and fills the lakes of Udaipur, which looks its best at this time. The crowds are gone and hotels are cheaper. The trade-offs are humidity, occasional heavy showers that can slow road travel, and the closure of the tiger reserve at Ranthambore. Desert camping is usually not at its best in the rains.

Booking tips for the peak season

  • Book at least one to two months ahead for November to February, and earlier for Christmas, New Year and festival dates.
  • Udaipur and Jaisalmer hotels fill first, so lock those in before the rest of the route.
  • Keep one flexible day in the itinerary in case fog or traffic delays a long drive.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best month to visit Rajasthan?

November and February are widely considered the best months, with clear skies and comfortable temperatures. December and January are also excellent, though nights are cold in the desert.

Is October a good time to visit Rajasthan?

Yes. October follows the monsoon, so the landscape is fresh and green. Days can still be warm, but evenings are pleasant and crowds and prices are lower than in peak winter.

Can I visit Rajasthan in the monsoon?

Yes. It is a quiet, cheaper season and Udaipur’s lakes look their best, but humidity and rain can affect outdoor plans, and Ranthambore National Park closes for the monsoon.

What is the best time for desert camping in Jaisalmer?

November to February offers cool, clear evenings for camel safaris and campfires. Pack warm clothing, as nights can be cold.

How far ahead should I book a Rajasthan trip?

For the peak season from November to February, book one to two months ahead. For festival dates and the New Year period, book even earlier.
