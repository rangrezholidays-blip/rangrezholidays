Jaipur Tour Package: 1, 2 and 3 Day Itineraries from Delhi

By Rangrez Holidays Travel Team · Updated October 17, 2026 · 5 min read

A Jaipur tour package is one of the most popular short trips from Delhi. The Pink City packs a hilltop fort, a royal palace, a wind-swept facade and an open-air observatory into a compact area, and it is only a few hours from the capital by road.

Below you will find one, two and three day Jaipur itineraries, the key sights with the time each one needs, how to get there, and what a private Jaipur tour costs.

How to get from Delhi to Jaipur

Jaipur is roughly 270 km from Delhi. By road it takes about five to six hours, and the newer expressways have shortened the drive. A private car gives you the most flexibility, since you can leave at dawn and stop on the way. Trains and flights are available too, but a car makes it simpler to combine several sights in one day.

2 day Jaipur itinerary

Day 1: forts and palaces

Reach Amber Fort early, before the crowds and the heat. Stop at Jal Mahal for photos, have lunch, then see the City Palace and Jantar Mantar. End the day in the old city bazaars, such as Johari Bazaar, for jewellery, block-printed textiles and blue pottery.

Day 2: viewpoints and culture

Photograph Hawa Mahal in the morning, when its east-facing facade catches the light. Visit the Albert Hall Museum, Galta Ji temple and the hilltop fort at Nahargarh for sunset. Finish with dinner at a cultural village-style restaurant such as Chokhi Dhani.

3 day Jaipur itinerary

Follow the two-day plan and use the third day to go slower or go further:

  • Handicrafts: visit Sanganer for block-printing and paper workshops.
  • Abhaneri stepwell: a striking medieval stepwell about 95 km from Jaipur, easy to combine on the road towards Agra.
  • Pushkar: a day trip to the holy lake town, or an overnight stop if you are continuing to Jodhpur.

Best time to visit Jaipur

October to March is the most comfortable period, with sunny days and cool evenings. Summers are very hot and the monsoon brings humidity. See our month-by-month Rajasthan guide for festivals such as the Jaipur Literature Festival and Teej.

Practical tips for visiting Jaipur

  • Start early at Amber Fort to beat the crowds and the midday heat.
  • Wear comfortable shoes. Forts have steep ramps and uneven stone.
  • Bargain politely in the bazaars and compare prices before buying.
  • Try local food: dal baati churma, laal maas and, in the monsoon, the sweet ghewar.
  • Keep small change for entry fees, tips and street snacks.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Jaipur?

Two days is ideal to see the main forts, palaces and bazaars without rushing. One day is possible if you start early, and three days lets you add Sanganer, a stepwell or Pushkar.

Can I do Jaipur in one day from Delhi?

Yes. A same-day trip by private car covers Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jal Mahal, but it is a long day of 14 to 16 hours.

How far is Jaipur from Delhi?

About 270 km. The road journey takes around five to six hours depending on traffic.

How much is a same day Jaipur tour from Delhi?

What is the best time to visit Jaipur?

October to March, when the weather is dry and pleasant.
